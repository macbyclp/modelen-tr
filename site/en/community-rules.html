Community

Community rules

This is a place for people working together on open AI. We keep it short.

Basic rules

  • We treat each other respectfully; harassment, hate speech, threats and belittling are not allowed.
  • Spam, bulk advertising and deceptive links are not allowed.
  • We do not share anyone else's personal data (phone, address, ID, private messages) without permission.
  • We do not share illegal content, copyright-infringing content or links to malware.
  • We do not knowingly spread misleading information or present model results as better than they are.
  • In your profile and posts you use only images you have the right to share.

Privacy

Your profile is private by default; nobody can see it until you open it. Your email address is never shown. You can block a user; a blocked person cannot see or follow you.

Reporting and moderation

You can report a post or profile that breaks the rules with the "Report" button. Admins review reports and may hide a post, hide a profile or restrict posting. To appeal a decision, write to iletisim@modelen.tr. Legal notices (e.g. rights infringement) go to the same address and are reviewed within a reasonable time.
