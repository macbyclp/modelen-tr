In Turkish AI, no one should hold this alone:

the code.

We will open the training code and the volunteer client under Apache-2.0.

the weights.

Model weights will be released so anyone can download and run them on their own machine.

the data list.

We will state plainly what we train on and the licence of every source.

the decisions.

Contributions will be on record; we will set the direction together.

Your graphics card is enough. No server room needed.

We freeze the base model and train only a small LoRA adapter. In our experiment peak memory was 5.2 GB; it fit on an 8 GB laptop card.

How it works
base ≈ 1.2 GB

We move less data. We get the same result.

Volunteers synchronise once every 25 steps, not every step. In our simulation about 25 times less data moved; the difference was 0.09% in bits per byte.

Why the Halay Ring?

In a halay everyone links arms with their neighbour; the circle has no beginning and no end. Our network will work the same way.

Every volunteer carries a lantern: the effort of your graphics card, a light. The light passes from hand to hand and no single hand holds it.

The circle
No leader; everyone stands in an equal place.
The hands
Contributions are linked and verify one another.
The lanterns
Each volunteer's light flows into the shared model.
